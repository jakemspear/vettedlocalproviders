Privacy Policy

Scaling Adventures, LLC d/b/a Casa Concierge explains in this Privacy Policy what personal information we collect, how we use it, how our SMS opt-in program works, and how users can exercise their privacy choices.

What information we collect

We may collect your name, phone number, email address, home address, service request details, scheduling preferences, homeowner status, and any information you submit through forms, calls, emails, or text messages.

We may also collect technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring page, cookies, pixels, and analytics data.

How we use collected data

We use personal information to review quote requests, schedule appointments, coordinate service providers, send service-related follow-up, provide customer support, measure marketing performance, and improve the website.

SMS/text message program and opt-in process

If you optionally opt in to receive SMS messages, we may send conversational, transactional, and promotional text messages related to your quote request, appointment scheduling, requested provider coordination, service updates, and related services you ask about.

We store the source of the opt-in, timestamp, page URL or lead form source, phone number, and the exact consent language shown at the time of opt-in. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. To stop receiving messages, reply STOP to any text message. Reply HELP for assistance.

Cookies and tracking technologies

We may use cookies, pixels, analytics tools, and similar technologies to understand website traffic, measure advertising, improve page performance, and remember user preferences.

Data security practices

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect personal information. No method of online transmission or storage is guaranteed to be completely secure.

User rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information where applicable. You may unsubscribe from marketing emails using the unsubscribe link in those messages. For text messages, reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

No sharing of mobile opt-in data

We do not share mobile information, phone numbers, SMS opt-in consent, or related messaging records with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be sold or shared with any third parties except vendors who help us operate the service.
